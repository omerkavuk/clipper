# Clipper setup wizard: the script

What the user sees, screen by screen. Rules for every screen:

- One short heading, at most two lines of text, then the question.
- Every question says, in one line starting with "→", what Clipper will do with the answer.
- Choices are clickable options (the question tool). Free text only where nothing can be offered.
- No commentary between screens. No file names, commands or technical words.

---

## Screen 0: Welcome

> **Clipper watches the media for your organisation and sends you a report you can forward.**
>
> It can watch:
> - **News sites**: national and regional papers, news agencies, TV and radio sites, trade press
> - **Think tanks, research centres, government and institutional sites**
> - **Social media**: LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Reddit
>
> Setup is 10 short questions. At the end I build your source list, test it on last week's news and show you the cost. Nothing runs until you approve it.

Options: **Start setup** · **What does a report look like?** (shows the example report, then returns here)

## Screen 1: Organisation

> **Which organisation should I watch?**
> → I'll search for this name, its translations and its acronym in every language you pick.

Free text.

## Screen 2: Country

> **Which country's media?**
> → At the end of setup I'll build a list of {country}'s news outlets, think tanks and institutions, and show it to you for approval.

Options: up to 3 likely countries (from the organisation's name or location), plus free text.

## Screen 3: Languages

> **Which languages does that press write in?**
> → I'll search in each language you tick.

Multi-select: the country's main language or languages (pre-ticked) · English · Other.

## Screen 4: Names to search

> **Which names should count as a mention?**
> → Each ticked name becomes a search. Untick any the press doesn't use.

Multi-select, pre-ticked, generated by Clipper: official name, each translation, acronym, common short forms. "Add another" opens free text.

## Screen 5: Programmes, events and people

> **Any programmes, events, products or spokespeople to follow?**
> → These usually find more coverage than the organisation's name. Names that are also everyday words are only counted next to your organisation's name.

Free text (optional). Options: **None** · **Type them**.

## Screen 6: Leave out

> **What should never appear in the report?**
> → Matching articles are dropped before the report is written.

Multi-select:
- Your own websites: {proposed domains} (pre-ticked)
- Your own social media accounts: {proposed handles} (pre-ticked)
- Job adverts, internships and calls for applications (pre-ticked)
- Other meanings of your names (type them)

## Screen 7: Types of sources

> **Which kinds of sources should I include?**
> → I'll put these in your {country} source list.

Multi-select, all pre-ticked except "Trade and specialist press":
National news · Regional and local news · News agencies · TV and radio · Trade and specialist press · Think tanks and research · Government and institutions

## Screen 8: Social media

> **Should I also watch social media?**
> → Platforms you pick are searched for posts that mention you. X, Instagram and Facebook need a one-time login in a private browser; I never see your password.

Multi-select: None · LinkedIn (public posts through web search; some will be missed) · X · Instagram · Facebook (each needs a one-time login) · YouTube · Reddit. TikTok can't be searched by date yet.

## Screen 10b: Social login (only for X, Instagram or Facebook)

> **X, Instagram and Facebook need a one-time setup.**
> → I'll install a private browser for Clipper (a few hundred MB). Then you log in once in the window that opens. Your own browser, accounts and other tools are not changed.

Options: **Set it up now** · **Skip these platforms**

## Screen 9: How often

> **How often do you want the report?**
> → Each report covers everything since the last one.

Options: **Weekly, on Monday morning** (default) · **Daily** · **Monthly**.

## Screen 10: Who reads it

> **Who will read the report?**
> → This sets how long the summary is. Reports are in English for now.

Options: **Just me** · **My team** · **Management** · **Clients or partners**.

---

## Screen 11: Your source list (Clipper works)

> **Building your {country} source list…**

Then:

> **{N} sources for {country}:**
> {n} national news · {n} regional · {n} agencies · {n} TV and radio · {n} think tanks · {n} government and institutions
> Plus: {social platforms}.
>
> Examples: {6 well-known names}

Options: **Looks good** · **Add sources** (free text: names or websites) · **Remove sources** · **See the full list**

## Screen 12: Test on last week

> **Checking last week's news…**

Then one line per search: "{name}: {n} articles", with a plain warning where needed ("finds nothing", "too common a word: I'll only count it next to your organisation's name", "mostly your own website"). Options: **Looks right** · **Fix a search** (shows the suggested fix) · **Test again**.

If nothing could be reached: "The news sources aren't answering right now. I'll try again in 30 minutes." No numbers are shown.

## Screen 13: Cost

> **Each {weekly} report uses about {N} tokens (about {M} a month).**
> → Most of it is reading and sorting the articles; the rest is writing the summary.

Options: **Approve** · **Make it cheaper** (fewer sources or stricter searches).

## Screen 14: Schedule

> **Send the report automatically every {Monday at 08:07}?**
> → It runs on this computer. If the computer is off, the next run covers the missed period.

Options: **Yes, schedule it** · **Not now: I'll ask for it**

## Screen 15: Done

> **You're set.** First report: {date}. Ask "run my media report" any time, or "media monitoring status".
